# Capability: Total Cost Display

**Description:** Every price shown to a traveler is the final price — no hidden cleaning fees, service fees, or checkout surprises.

**Bundles:** b1

**User Story:**
As a traveler, I want to see the total cost of my stay on the search results page, so I can compare options without clicking into each listing and doing math.

**Scope:**
- Search result cards show total cost for selected dates (not per-night)
- Listing page shows total cost prominently (largest text)
- No separate line items for cleaning, service fees, or platform fees
- Hosts set one all-inclusive nightly rate
- Taxes shown separately (required by law) but clearly labeled
- Price breakdown available on tap: (nightly rate × nights) + taxes = total

**Out of Scope:**
- Dynamic/algorithmic pricing suggestions to hosts (never)
- Lowest-price sorting as default (amenities-first)

**Related Capabilities:**
- Traveler Search & Browse
- Booking Flow
