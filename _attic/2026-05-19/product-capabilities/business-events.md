# Capability: Business Events

**Description:** Business owners can post events happening at or hosted by their business

**Bundles:** b1

**User Story:**
As a business owner, I want to post upcoming events, so that community members can discover what's happening at my business.

**Scope:**
- Create event: title, description, date, start time, end time
- Location defaults to business address
- Events appear in consumer feed and on business profile
- Optional: cost (free/paid), recurring flag
- Past events auto-archive
- Business owner can edit or cancel

**Out of Scope:**
- RSVP / attendance tracking (b2)
- Calendar sync (b2)
- Recurring event creation UI (b2)
- Event photos (b2)

**Related Capabilities:**
- Business Registration (owner must be registered)
- Consumer Feed (events appear here)
- Business Profile (events listed on profile)
