# Capability: Neighborhood Context

**Description:** Each listing shows computed neighborhood data — walkability, transit, STR density, noise indicators — so travelers understand the area and communities can see rental concentration.

**Bundles:** b2

**User Story:**
As a traveler, I want to understand the neighborhood around a rental — walkability, noise, transit — without relying on the host's description alone.

**Scope:**
- Walkability score (computed from OSM data)
- Transit proximity and options
- Short-term rental density: count of STRs within 0.5 miles
- Rental-to-resident ratio for the block/neighborhood
- Noise indicators: airport, highway, nightlife district proximity
- Data shown on listing page in a "Neighborhood" section

**Out of Scope:**
- Crime statistics (liability concerns)
- School ratings (not relevant to short-term stays)
- Community veto trigger (T3)

**Related Capabilities:**
- Mandatory Disclosures
- Community Impact Score
