# Capability: Multi-Unit Friction Gate

**Description:** Adding a second property triggers manual review and justification. Three or more properties are not supported.

**Bundles:** b1

**User Story:**
As a platform, I want to make listing multiple properties intentionally difficult, so that operators with dozens of rentals use other platforms and our host community stays local and small.

**Scope:**
- First listing: standard onboarding (fast, frictionless)
- Second listing: requires written justification (inherited property, ADU, etc.), enters 48-hour manual review queue
- Third+ listing: blocked with explanation ("This platform is designed for hosts with one or two properties")
- Duplicate detection: address similarity, photo fingerprinting, listing language patterns

**Out of Scope:**
- Property management company accounts (never supported)
- Automated approval for second units (always manual)

**Related Capabilities:**
- Host Onboarding
- Host Verification
