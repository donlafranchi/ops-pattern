# Capability: Locality Transparency

**Description:** Businesses display transparent locality facts (owner, location, tenure) and a simple color derived from those facts

**Bundles:** b1

**User Story:**
As a consumer, I want to see at a glance whether a business is locally owned and how long they've been in my community, so I can choose to keep my money circulating locally.

**Scope:**
- Registration collects: owner name, owner city, years at location, number of locations, franchise/chain status, investment firm ownership
- Business profile displays these facts plainly (not scored, not judged)
- Pin color on map derived from facts: green (local owner, in community), amber (regional/franchise), gray (corporate/PE/absentee)
- Color assignment is deterministic from registration answers, not curated

**Out of Scope:**
- Verification of locality claims (b2)
- "Verified Local" badge for long-tenure businesses (b2)
- Automated verification from public records (b3)

**Related Capabilities:**
- Business Registration (facts collected here)
- Map Search (pin colors)
- Consumer Feed (badge on cards)
- Business Profile (facts displayed)
