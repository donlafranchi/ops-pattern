# Capability: Business Promotions

**Description:** Business owners can create time-limited promotions to drive foot traffic and visibility

**Bundles:** b1

**User Story:**
As a business owner, I want to post promotions with start/end dates, so that nearby customers can discover my deals and visit my shop.

**Scope:**
- Create promotion: title, description, start date, end date
- Optional: discount amount, promo code
- Promotion appears in consumer feed sorted by proximity + recency
- Countdown/expiration indicator on promotion card
- Auto-archive after end date (no longer visible in feed)
- Business owner can edit or end a promotion early
- Promotions visible on business profile page

**Out of Scope:**
- Photo attachments on promotions (b2)
- Push notifications for new promotions (b2)
- Analytics on promotion views/engagement (b2)
- Recurring/repeating promotions (b2)

**Related Capabilities:**
- Business Registration (owner must be registered)
- Consumer Feed (promotions appear here)
- Business Profile (promotions listed on profile)
