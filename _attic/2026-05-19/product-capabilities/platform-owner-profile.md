# Capability: Owner Profile

**Description:** A single profile shared across all listing types — whether someone lists a business, a rental, a farm, or all three, they have one identity on the platform.

**Bundles:** b1

**Platform Layer:** Core

**User Story:**
As a local business owner who also rents my guest house and sells eggs from my farm, I want one profile that shows everything I do in this community, so customers and travelers see the whole picture.

**Scope:**
- Display name, photo, bio
- Contact email, phone (optional), social media links
- Local residency: city/county where the person lives
- "Member since" date
- Role indicators: business owner, host, producer (one person can hold multiple)
- Profile page aggregates all listings across categories
- "Local" badge if person lives in same city/county as their listing(s)

**Out of Scope:**
- Verified identity badge (T2 — requires ID check)
- Response rate metrics (T2)
- Cross-category reputation score (T2)

**Related Capabilities:**
- Business Registration (creates profile during core signup)
- Host Onboarding (extends profile when Stays vertical is used)
- Community Trust signals (displayed on profile)
