# Capability: Map Search

**Description:** Consumers can browse a map to find local businesses, visually distinguished by ownership type

**Bundles:** b1

**User Story:**
As a consumer, I want to see businesses on a map colored by ownership type, so that I can find independent businesses near me.

**Scope:**
- Full-screen interactive map with colored pins
- Tap pin to see business detail card (slides up from bottom)
- Geolocation for initial map position
- Pin clustering at low zoom
- Bottom navigation bar modeled after Google Maps / Apple Maps
- Search bar (anchored to bottom): search by business category and/or location
- Search results filter visible pins on the map
- All primary controls at bottom of viewport (thumb-friendly, mobile-first)

**Out of Scope:**
- Filters by ownership type (b2)
- Saved locations (b2)
- Search history (b2)

**Related Capabilities:**
- Business Detail View
- Business Registration (provides data)
