## Story

Marcus makes hot sauce and also hosts a monthly swap — one person, two Pages. Today only a business Page has a public URL; his swap's page 404s, and the app assumes he has exactly one Page. Every surface that lists "his shop" instead lists every Page he holds, and the swap's page renders like any other.

## Acceptance

1. A non-business Page's public URL renders (name, description, image, what's filed under it) instead of 404ing.
2. Products, services, and gatherings filed under a non-business Page resolve at their public URL.
3. A Member holding several Pages sees every one on any producer surface — none silently chosen for them.
4. Draft-resume keeps every in-flight draft, not just the most recent.

## Not this

A switch-context surface. Aggregating messages or activity across a Member's several Pages — parked, not scoped.
