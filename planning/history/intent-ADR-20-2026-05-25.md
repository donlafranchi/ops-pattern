## Category-2 absolutes — annotated, substantive

### 1. ADR-20:167 — Category 2 (refusal / commitment)

> "URLs are locality-scoped wherever a place is the most stable anchor."

**Existing Intent.** Substantive — names the failure mode ("rewriting every public URL the platform has ever issued, which destroys the durability of share links and SEO") and gives the disposition for edge cases ("we can always extend; we can never quietly un-commit"). Downstream agent can break ties: if a resource has *no* place anchor (Member-owned Items, the Member handle itself), it falls outside locality-scoping by design — handle-anchored URLs are the explicit complement.

**Load-bearing?** Yes. Forgetting this would silently contradict `principles.md` P1 (locality-first) and the place-scoped routing in `places.md` § URL hierarchy.

### 2. ADR-20:170 — Category 2 (refusal / structural commitment)

> "Members keep a single global handle."

**Existing Intent.** Substantive — names the failure mode ("scoping handles to places would force handle changes on every relocation, destroying identity continuity") and gives the disposition ("handles are short, profanity-filtered, regex-constrained" — the cost is bounded; the global namespace is earned).

**Load-bearing?** Yes. Forgetting this would silently contradict the auth-identity equality in ADR-15 (members.auth_pk = users.id) and the durable-relocation premise that runs through `member.md`.

### 3. ADR-20:173 — Category 2 (refusal / curation policy)

> "Places are platform-curated, not user-created."

**Existing Intent.** Substantive — names the failure mode ("allowing user-created 'cities' would let one person fork the entire URL namespace by declaring their block a city") and gives the disposition for user-declared geographic scopes ("`locations.kind='area'` — service areas, custom polygons — distinct from `places`").

**Load-bearing?** Yes. Forgetting this would silently contradict `places.md` § What a Place Is and Why It Matters (curation policy) and the URL-namespace stability commitment.
