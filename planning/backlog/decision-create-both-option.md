# Decision — does "Both" survive the many-Pages ruling?

> **RULED 2026-09-07: Branch A. "Both" is dropped.** Two answers in the create flow; someone who does both makes a second Page later, never at the same time. The *start another* line is part of the work, not a nicety — it is recorded as an acceptance criterion on the scenario rather than left as a recommendation.

**Raised:** 2026-09-07, by the many-Pages ruling. **Blocks:** the entry-point scenario reaching a ticket. **Nothing is written into the scenario until this is ruled.**

## The question in one line

The create question was designed when one person got one Page. **With many Pages, "Both" is either a Page that does two things, or it is the wrong option and the answer is two Pages.**

## Recommendation — drop "Both"

**It follows from the rulings already made rather than adding anything new.**

- **Selling and hosting are two different things with two different creation processes.** The business path carries verification the host path does not — that is where the friction was deliberately put.
- **A single Page doing both means business friction leaks onto the host side.** That is the exact inversion that was just fixed: the run-club organiser answering business questions to convene a run.
- **The create question becomes two answers, then the name.** Something I make or sell · Something I host.
- **Someone who does both makes a second Page.** Under many-Pages that is the ordinary path, not a consolation prize.

### The cost, stated honestly

**A market vendor who also hosts a monthly meetup manages two Pages instead of one.** That is real friction landing on exactly the small producer the platform exists to protect, and it is the strongest argument for keeping "Both."

**The mitigation is what makes the recommendation safe, and it is not optional.** After a Page is created, the flow offers *start another* in a line — one tap, straight back into the question with the name step ready. **Not a redirect to the beginning.** If that line is cut for time, the recommendation gets materially worse and should be revisited rather than shipped thin.

## Branch A — drop "Both" *(recommended)*

- Create question carries **two** answers, then the name step.
- No Page is ever both; a person who does both holds two Pages.
- A *start another* affordance appears at the end of creation and on the producer surface.
- **Scenario edits:** the three-way question becomes two-way in § The Story and § Surfaces; the answer table loses its *Both* row; the acceptance criterion *"One person, two verbs, one Page"* is **replaced** by one asserting that a second Page is one tap away and that the first is untouched by the second.
- **Ticket impact:** none beyond copy and one affordance. The choice step is a two-option radio group instead of three.

## Branch B — keep "Both"

- Create question carries three answers, as currently written.
- *Both* creates one Page carrying a business claim, which can also host.
- **The problem to solve if this branch is taken:** the business questions must not be asked of the hosting half. Either the claim is deferred until the first sale, or the flow asks them once and the host side visibly does not use them. **Neither has been designed.**
- **Scenario edits:** none — the scenario as written already describes this branch.
- **Ticket impact:** the deferred-claim question needs answering before the ticket, which puts a design question back in front of the work.

## What decides it

**Whether the friction on the business path is worth avoiding twice.** If the business questions are light, "Both" is convenient and harmless. If they are heavy — and the verification ladder is designed to be heavy — then a combined Page either drags that weight onto hosting or defers it, and deferral is an undesigned mechanic.

**Recommendation stands: Branch A, with the *start another* line treated as part of the work rather than a nicety.**
