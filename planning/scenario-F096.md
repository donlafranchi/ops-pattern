## Story

Jess follows six venues, three bands and a run club across four apps just to know what's on this weekend. The Torch Club links its Instagram and calendar to its SocialUs Page once, saying yes to it, and its shows appear on its Page and in Explore, each marked as from the Torch Club's own calendar with a link back to it. Jess hears about a pop-up at a gallery that isn't on SocialUs yet and shares it; it shows as shared by a member, not confirmed, and the gallery is invited to claim it. When the gallery claims it, the mark goes. Jess stops following everyone everywhere, because SocialUs recommends from what she said she likes before things happen.

## Acceptance

1. **An owner links an Instagram account or a calendar to their Page once, with explicit consent, and can unlink it;** unlinking stops new imports.
2. **Every imported event names its source and links back to the owner's own site or channel.**
3. **A member can share an event they heard about;** it is marked unconfirmed until its venue claims it, and the venue is invited to.
4. **Nothing is gathered from a public listing without the owner's consent.**
5. **Imported and shared events follow the same visibility as any announcement,** including the signed-out front door (F093).

## Not this

Not launch. Not approved. No scraping of listings without consent (C). No ticketing or booking. Not a copy of the venue's site.

## Why

**Recommend for members before things happen,** from their declared interests: the member works with the platform, not an algorithm (Don, 2026-09-30). It sits inside the existing ordering rule — place, declared interests and genuine community response (`product/ui/surfaces.md` § ordering; DECISIONS 2026-09-12) — and ranks nobody for payment, size or follower count (2026-09-30).

**Links back are the owner's own:** a venue's consented connection counts as links the owner typed, so F090 criterion 7 holds (Don, 2026-09-30).
