## Story

Right now, nobody can turn this on. Every member, regardless of tenure, is blocked from posting a photo or listing primarily about a child — not gated behind a setting, just off. The unlock (a verified-identity tier — ID plus a selfie match) doesn't exist yet; when it does, only a member who's completed it can.

## Acceptance

1. Any post whose image or text is classified as primarily about or depicting a child is rejected at submission with a plain reason, for every member.
2. There is no toggle, request, or workaround that lets a Tier 0 (self-attested) member post this content today.
3. Anything that slips past submission-time is caught by F078's same agent-flag mechanism.

## Not this

Building the ID/selfie verification tier itself — separate, unscheduled work; this scenario ships without it existing. Defining exactly what "primarily about a child" means for the classifier beyond the confirmed scope (about/depicting, not any mention) — that's Code's tuning work.
