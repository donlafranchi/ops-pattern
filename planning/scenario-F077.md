## Story

Rae signs up with her legal name and email; the platform records it and never asks her to show it to anyone. She picks a display name — "Rae," a nickname, whatever she wants — and that's what every other member sees on her posts, her Page, her responses. If she ever does something worth reporting, the operator opens her record and sees the legal name behind "Rae," not just a handle.

## Acceptance

1. Signup requires a full legal name field, separate from email; the account can't be created without it.
2. A member sets a display name, shown everywhere a peer would otherwise see their legal name — posts, Pages, responses, profile.
3. Legal name is never rendered anywhere a peer member or anonymous visitor can reach — profile, listing, search, map popup.
4. The operator's report-review view (F058) shows the reported member's legal name alongside their display name.
5. Existing seeded/test members are backfilled with a legal name or flagged before this ships to real signups.

## Not this

ID or document verification — self-attested is enough at launch. Any peer-facing surface showing the legal name. Retroactively verifying legal names already on file.
