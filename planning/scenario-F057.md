## Story

Devon signed up because a neighbour sent a link; he makes hot sauce but hasn't listed anything. He taps You and sees his own stuff — what he follows, his settings — and, filling the page's second half, an invitation naming specific kinds of maker near him with one worked example. He taps Start something. Ten minutes later he's a producer, and You shows his shop instead of the invitation.

## Acceptance

1. `/you` contains no occurrence of "vendor," "market," or "business owner," and queries none of the seven dead tables.
2. A Member with no active Page sees no shop controls — only their own stuff and one invitation.
3. The invitation names specific local maker categories (not just selling), with one worked example.
4. A Member with ≥1 Page sees a row per Page (Edit shop + create controls) and their listings, reusing the existing query.
5. `/following` redirects to `/you/following`; sign-out and the follow list behave exactly as shipped.

## Not this

The Explore→Home nav fold. A "become a producer" toggle that makes someone pick a class for themselves. Deleting vendor-era files.
