System design · Human judgment document

Statute & precedent — for one human

Three layers that change at different rates, refined by a four-step loop. Same shape as common law: principles up top, cases at the bottom, with promotion in between.

The three layers

Layer 1
Intent
One paragraph per topic answering "what is this domain for, and what would I never trade away?" The non-negotiables and the spirit. Written in your voice, not in rules.
Tempo Rarely changes. Years.
Layer 2
In & out of bounds
Explicit defaults — and just as explicit out-of-bounds. The negative space is load-bearing: without it the agent can't tell "covered, decide alone" from "gap, escalate."
Tempo Slow. Months.
Layer 3
Casebook
Specific judgments rendered, dated, with one-line rationale. The agent logs every decision it makes; you log every escalation you answer. When N cases on one topic show a pattern, promote the pattern up to Layer 2.
Tempo Grows constantly.

The refinement loop

Encounter
Identify topic
Agent classifies the situation against the topic manifest. Loads only that topic's three layers.
Apply
Try layers 1–2
If bounds cover it, agent decides and logs as a case in Layer 3. No escalation. You don't see it.
Escalate (only if gap)
Gap diagnosis
If bounds don't cover it, agent surfaces: topic, closest existing case, what's different now, recommendation. Not "what should I do" — "here's what isn't covered."
Refine
Promote or just log
Your answer becomes a new case. You signal: just log it, or promote to Layer 2 as a new default or boundary. The doc grows from the gap.

Worked example — one topic, fully populated

Topic file · vendor-and-cost.md

Vendor selection & recurring spend

Layer 1 — Intent
Predictable cost structure beats marginal cost optimization. Operational simplicity beats vendor diversity. I'll pay a premium for managed services until the bill itself becomes a forcing function for change.
Layer 2 — In bounds
Managed services under $X/mo: agent picks based on integration fit and documentation quality. Open-source alternatives for foundational infra. Sole-source vendor lock-in acceptable for non-foundational tooling.
Layer 2 — Out of bounds
Anything that requires me to read a contract over $Y/year. Vendors without a clear exit path on customer data. Tools that route my workflow through a closed agentic system I can't inspect.
Case · 2026-03-14
Picked Voyage AI embeddings over self-hosted sentence-transformers. Rationale: managed beat marginal-cost-savings; ~$12/mo at current volume.
Case · 2026-04-02
Escalated FWS Platinum extended warranty cancel/keep — out of bounds (recurring spend, exit-path question). Don ruled: cancel. Promote? No — too specific.
Case · 2026-05-10
Escalated: candidate vendor uses closed agent framework. Don ruled: out of bounds. Promote? Yes — added to Layer 2 out-of-bounds above.
Read this design as: the doc isn't written upfront — it's grown from use. Start with one-line intents per topic and let cases accumulate. The agent's logging is the substrate; your judgment on what promotes is the editorial work. Over time, escalations get rarer because the bounds get sharper.
