# T101 — F039 Playwright eval + fixture

## 2026-06-04 — Two deviations

1. **F038 eval beat 4 forward-fixed (cross-feature edit).** F038's beat 4 ("Locally Made badge is data-gated") asserted the `product-made-badge` renders for an **anonymous** viewer on a `made_at`-set product. Under F039 the badge is **proximity-gated** (renders only for a viewer whose `member_place_interests` are local to the made-at Place), so an anon viewer never earns it — the prior assertion would now fail. The beat is re-pointed to assert the always-public `product-made-in` ("Made in {place}") line instead, which preserves its original intent ("the skip-path absence is data-driven, not a dead render path") and adds an explicit "anon → no badge" assertion. F038 eval still 8/8 GREEN. This is a legitimate forward-fix for a contract F039 deliberately changes, not a regression.

2. **Beat 1 (composer create) covered at the component + owner-widget layers, not by driving the multi-step composer blind.** Per the F038 coverage note, driving the multi-step composer UI in an eval is fragile (pickup picker renders from a stubbed location set). F039 beat 1 ("Maya picks a made-at Place during create") is covered by (a) the `ProductComposer` vitest that drives the made-step picker → asserts `madeAtPlaceId` flows to `createProduct`, and (b) the owner-widget Add path in the F039 eval, which exercises the same `item.made_at.set` handler end-to-end on a live DB. The non-proximal viewer's lineage (Los Angeles) is **seeded by the fixture** — LA is not in the b1 Sacramento-region seed, so the fixture inserts an LA county+city off CA to give a genuinely off-lineage interest.
