About

About this mockup

  • Scope: T1 / b1 only — three tabs (Home, Explore, You), one Item detail, and the Sell composer drawer. No T2 surfaces (Bulletin, RSVP, Producer panel), no auth, no Venue/Member/Group pages.
  • Bottom-anchored per ADR-2: tab bar, search bar (on Explore), primary CTAs (sticky on Item detail), and the composer (slides up from the bottom). Tap "Sell something" on You to feel it.
  • One accent color: Tide green #0FAB8E on primary CTAs, the brand mark, and the locality-pill indicator. Hairlines, not shadows, at rest.
  • Content: West Sac and Sacramento — Sutter Buttes Sourdough, the Saturday market, Concerts in the Park, Tower Bridge Run Club, Drake's, a real-shaped Idea.

What to look at

  • Home feed — the five-card-type mix (Featured, Event, Idea, Seller update, plus more Events). Photography-led; hairline cards; no shadows at rest.
  • Explore — search bar at the bottom, not the top. Filter chips float just above it. The list scrolls behind both.
  • You — locality is implicit in the handle row, not a separate header pill (we'll see if that holds up). The Sell CTA is the loudest thing on the page; everything else is settings-quiet.
  • Item detail — back/share are floating glassy buttons over the hero. The primary action is sticky at the bottom with a trust line underneath ("No fees, ever.").
  • Sell composer — four steps, dots at top, Back + Continue at bottom, picker rows for the kind-of-shop choice. Single source of truth for any composer; the gathering/product/service composers should reuse this shell.

Open for discussion

  • Locality control placement on You — implicit in the handle, or surfaced as its own row? Both fight the principle of "calm, settings-shaped page."
  • "Sell something" copy — does it feel right, or do we want a verb that matches more kinds (host, list, share)?
  • Card visual treatment — are SVG placeholders honest enough to evaluate, or do you want stock-photo URLs swapped in?
  • Explore chip row — is 7 chips too many for a one-thumb sweep?
