West Sac Farmers Market

📅 Saturday, June 13 · 8:00 AM – 1:00 PM
📍 Bridge District Plaza · 0.4 mi away
🔁 Repeats every Saturday

About

Every Saturday morning year-round in the Bridge District. Around 35 producers from within 50 miles — sourdough, stone fruit, honey, eggs, a few prepared-food stalls, live music after 10am. Bring a tote.

Final mockup — design direction

  • Grey cards for no-photo items. Ideas & Asks use #F0F0F0 grey backgrounds — editorial, typographic, zero color. In the Explore list, no-photo items get a grey thumbnail with uppercase kind label + author initial.
  • Pistachio used liberally, on white only. Brand color (#BACBB5) appears on: logo dots, locality-pill pin, active filter chips, "Show all" links (darker pistachio-700), section accent lines, Featured badge outline, selected-state borders in the sell drawer, step-progress dots, input focus ring, map pin on detail page, and detail kind label. CTA buttons use pistachio-500 (#A0B49A) fill with white text.
  • Pistachio never on color. The promo/sell CTA card uses white background (with pistachio left-border accent) so the pistachio CTA button sits on white. No pistachio on grey cards, no pistachio on photo cards, no pistachio on tinted surfaces.
  • Photo cards are sacred. No overlays, no pills on images. The only overlay is the heart icon (white stroke + shadow). Featured badge uses a pistachio-outlined white pill — visible but not polluting the photo.
  • Dark neutral text (#2D2D2D). Body and headings in near-black. Muted text in #717171. No brand-colored text.
  • White canvas. Pure white page background. Grey (#E8E8E8) outer frame only.
  • Phone frame: Near-black (#1A1A1A). Not brand-colored.

Pistachio surface inventory

  • Logo dots: #BACBB5 (pistachio-400)
  • Locality pin: #BACBB5
  • Section accent lines: #BACBB5 (32px × 2px)
  • "Show all" links: #6D8367 (pistachio-700 — passes AA for text)

  • Featured badge: white bg + #BACBB5 border + #6D8367 text
  • CTA buttons: #A0B49A fill (pistachio-500) + white text
  • Promo card: white bg + #BACBB5 left border
  • Active nav icon: #6D8367 (pistachio-700)
