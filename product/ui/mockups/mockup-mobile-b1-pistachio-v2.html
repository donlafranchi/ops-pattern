About

Every Saturday morning year-round in the Bridge District. Around 35 producers from within 50 miles — sourdough, stone fruit, honey, eggs, a few prepared-food stalls, live music after 10am. Bring a tote.

v2 changes (Airbnb card analysis applied)

  • 1. Unified silhouette: Text cards (idea, seller update) now have a colored banner header that mirrors the photo position — same card width, same visual rhythm. Cards read as "top block + bottom text" regardless of type.
  • 2. Tags off images: Kind labels moved to a .kind-line in the text zone with a colored dot indicator. Photos are now clean — no overlaid pills or badges.
  • 3. Card containment: Every card has border: 1px solid var(--color-border) plus a warm green-tinted two-layer shadow (--shadow-card). Cards read as distinct objects.
  • 4. Accent reduced to two places: Pistachio appears on CTA buttons and the active bottom-nav tab. Stripped from: logo mark dots (→ border color), locality pin (→ foreground), You avatar (→ neutral gray), sell CTA block (→ surface), detail-kind label (→ muted).
  • 5. Beefier text: Third line added — host/seller attribution with a micro-avatar and social proof (follower count, member count, "going" count). Title dropped from 17px to 16px for tighter pairing with meta.
  • 6. Spacing: Inter-card gap bumped from 20px to 28px. Border-radius bumped from 12px to 14px.
  • 7. Carousel dots: Every photo card has white pagination dots at the bottom of the image. Signals "swipeable content" even in the static mockup.

What to look at

  • Home feed rhythm: Scroll the home tab. The tall-short seesaw is gone — text cards now have a banner header that fills the "image slot." Every card is a contained object with a border.
  • Clean photos: No more frosted-glass tags floating on images. The kind indicator is a small dot + label in the text zone.
  • Color discipline: The feed is now almost monochrome — photos and author banner colors provide the visual interest. Pistachio green appears only when you're about to tap something (the sell CTA, the share CTA, the active tab).
  • Text block weight: Three lines of info under each photo card. The attribution line (micro-avatar + name + count) adds social context and visual mass.

Flags

  • Text card banners: Using the author's color as a gradient banner is opinionated — it could feel heavy if the colors are dark. With real data, some banners may need a lighter treatment. An alternative: use var(--color-surface) with a left-border accent stripe.
  • Logo mark nearly invisible: At var(--color-border), the m·m·s dots are barely there. This is deliberate brand understatement per the accent-reduction goal. If it's too quiet, bump to var(--color-fg-muted).
  • Carousel dots are static: In a real implementation these would paginate on swipe. Here they're visual indicators only.
  • Photo card total height increased slightly: The third meta line adds ~18px per card. Offset by the 28px spacing creating more visual separation.
