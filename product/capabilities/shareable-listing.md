# Capability: Shareable Listing

**Description:** Each business has a unique URL with SSR metadata that can be shared on social media

**Bundles:** b1

**User Story:**
As a business owner, I want a shareable link to my listing, so that I can post it on social media and drive customers to my page.

**Scope:**
- SSR detail page at `/business/{slug}`
- OpenGraph metadata (title, description, ownership badge image)
- Mobile-responsive detail view

**Out of Scope:**
- Custom OG images per business (b2)
- Embed widgets for external sites (b3)

**Related Capabilities:**
- Business Detail View
- Business Registration
