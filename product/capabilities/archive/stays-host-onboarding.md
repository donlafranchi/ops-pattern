# Capability: Host Onboarding (Single Unit)

**Description:** A host can register one rental property with identity verification, photos, amenities, disclosures, and pricing — all in one session.

**Bundles:** b1

**User Story:**
As a local homeowner, I want to list my property quickly and honestly, so that travelers can find my place and know exactly what they're getting.

**Scope:**
- Natural person identity verification (no business entities)
- Property address, type, guest capacity
- Photo upload with geo-tag verification
- Structured amenity checklist + freeform custom amenities
- Mandatory disclosure form (noise, construction, shared spaces, distances)
- All-inclusive nightly rate (no separate fees)
- Cancellation policy selection
- Host profile (name, photo, bio, local residency)

**Out of Scope:**
- Multi-unit onboarding (separate capability with friction)
- Payment setup (part of Booking Flow)
- Calendar management (T3)

**Related Capabilities:**
- Multi-Unit Friction Gate
- Listing Creation
- Host Verification
