# Capability: Cross-Category Links

**Description:** Listings in one category can reference listings in another — a rental's amenities link to local businesses, a farm's products link to restaurants that serve them, a business page shows nearby rentals.

**Bundles:** b2

**Platform Layer:** Core (activated when first vertical launches)

**User Story:**
As a rental host, I want to credit the local roaster whose coffee I stock and the bike shop whose cruisers I lend, so my guests discover those businesses and those owners get visibility.

**Scope:**
- Stays amenity showcase can link to core business listings ("Coffee from [Local Roaster]" → business detail page)
- Stays local recommendations link to core business listings
- Harvest producer can link to restaurants/shops that carry their products
- Business detail page shows "Stay nearby" or "Local producers" sections
- Owner profile shows all listings across categories
- "This host recommends N independent businesses" signal on stays listing

**Out of Scope:**
- Affiliate/commission relationships between listings (never — no pay-to-play)
- Automated cross-linking (all links are owner-curated)

**Related Capabilities:**
- Amenity Showcase (Stays vertical)
- Local Recommendations (Stays vertical)
- Business Detail View (core)
- Owner Profile (core)
