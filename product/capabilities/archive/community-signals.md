# Capability: Community Signals (Support + Report)

**Description:** Users can support a business with a heart, or report a concern with a reason and explanation

**Bundles:** b1 (support + report submit), b2 (pillar tagging, standing scale, business dashboard)

**User Story:**
As a consumer, I want to show my support for a business or report a concern about its behavior, so that the community can see which businesses are worth supporting and hold bad actors accountable.

**Scope (b1):**
- ❤️ Support button on detail card (one tap, toggle on/off)
- Aggregate support count visible on listing
- "Report a concern" button
- Report form: select pillar (Customers, Employees, Community, Planet), brief description, optional source link
- Reports submitted privately — never displayed individually
- Basic admin view of report volume

**Scope (b2):**
- Optional pillar tag on support ("I support because of how they treat employees")
- Business standing scale (Questionable ↔ Exemplary) driven by support vs. verified concerns
- Business owner dashboard: endorsement trends, report pillar breakdown
- Report tracking IDs for submitters

**Out of Scope:**
- Text reviews or public comments (never)
- Star ratings (never)
- Check-ins or visit tracking (never)

**Related Capabilities:**
- Business Detail View (support count + standing indicator displayed here)
- Business Registration (standing starts neutral for new listings)
