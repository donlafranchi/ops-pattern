# Capability: Business Detail View

**Description:** Users can view a business listing with ownership badge, story, and support count

**Bundles:** b1

**User Story:**
As a consumer, I want to tap a map pin and see the business name, ownership status, and story, so that I can decide whether to visit.

**Scope:**
- Detail card overlay on map (mobile-first)
- Business name, address, category
- Ownership tier badge with color and label
- PE listings show parent company + location count
- Owner story / about text
- Shareable URL (SSR detail page with OG metadata)

**Out of Scope:**
- Photos (b2)
- Hours, phone, website (b2)
- Visit tracking or check-ins (never)

**Related Capabilities:**
- Map Search
- Community Signals
- Shareable Listing
