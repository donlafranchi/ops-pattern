# Capability: Mandatory Disclosures

**Description:** Hosts must disclose known issues and conditions before publishing. Disclosures are structured, visible, and auditable via reviews.

**Bundles:** b1

**User Story:**
As a traveler, I want to know about noise, construction, shared spaces, and real distances before I book, so I'm not surprised on arrival.

**Scope:**
- Structured disclosure form during listing creation:
  - Nearby construction (yes/no + details)
  - Noise sources (airport, highway, nightlife, neighbors)
  - Shared spaces (walls, entrance, yard, laundry)
  - Access limitations (stairs, parking, narrow roads)
  - Distance to key landmarks (computed from address, not host-claimed)
- Disclosures displayed prominently on listing page (not buried in fine print)
- Guest reviews include "Was the listing accurate?" prompt
- Disclosure accuracy tracked; repeated inaccuracy flags the listing

**Out of Scope:**
- Automated noise monitoring (T3 consideration)
- Legal compliance advice for hosts

**Related Capabilities:**
- Host Onboarding
- Verified Photos & Distance Audit
- Guest Reviews (T2 — not yet defined as capability file)
