# Capability: Ask a Question

**Description:** Consumers can ask questions directly to a business, with both question and response visible on the business profile

**Bundles:** b2

**User Story:**
As a consumer, I want to ask a business a question and see their answer publicly, so I can learn about them without relying on anonymous reviews.

**Scope:**
- Consumer submits a question (identified, not anonymous)
- Question goes to business owner
- Business owner can respond (response appears on profile)
- Both question and answer are public on the business profile
- Business can choose not to answer (silence is visible)
- No editing after posting (prevents gaslighting)

**Out of Scope:**
- Threaded replies / follow-ups (b3)
- Moderation tools for business owners (b3)
- Question voting / sorting (b3)

**Related Capabilities:**
- Business Profile (Q&A displayed here)
- Community Signals (questions are a form of engagement)
