# Capability: Business Updates

**Description:** Business owners can post general updates, news, and announcements

**Bundles:** b1

**User Story:**
As a business owner, I want to share news and updates with my community, so customers stay informed about my business.

**Scope:**
- Create update: title, body text
- Updates appear in consumer feed and on business profile
- Sorted by recency
- Business owner can edit or delete
- No expiration (persists until deleted)

**Out of Scope:**
- Photo attachments (b2)
- Rich text / markdown formatting (b2)
- Pinned/featured updates (b2)

**Related Capabilities:**
- Business Registration (owner must be registered)
- Consumer Feed (updates appear here)
- Business Profile (updates listed on profile)
