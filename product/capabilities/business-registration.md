# Capability: Business Registration

**Description:** Business owners can register their business and go live on the map in under 5 minutes

**Bundles:** b1

**User Story:**
As a business owner, I want to list my business with my ownership story, so that customers who value independence can find me.

**Scope:**
- Registration form: name, address, category, ownership type, story
- Ownership type selector (6 tiers)
- Address geocoding to lat/lng
- Pin goes live on map immediately after submission
- Basic Supabase email auth for owner account

**Out of Scope:**
- Photo uploads (b2)
- Business hours / contact info (b2)
- Claim existing listing (b2)
- Bulk registration (b3)

**Related Capabilities:**
- Map Search (pin appears)
- Business Detail View (data displayed)
- Shareable Listing (URL generated)
