# People — who Movers, Makers & Shakers serves

> **Status: DRAFT 2026-05-22.** First synthesis from `use-cases.md` and the b1 scenarios (`F018-brian-declares-run-club.md`, `F025-adaeze-member-public-page.md`). PM to refine — persona set, names, and emphasis not yet ratified. **[PM: confirm]** the persona list, the names, the use-case mapping, and whether any personas should be merged or split.

## How to read this

One section per persona. A real kind of person, not a market segment. Each persona is a *role someone is playing right now* — most real Members will play several over time, but at any given moment they are usually in one. The platform's job is to serve whichever role they're in without making them claim a permanent identity.

Names are illustrative. Where a use case in `use-cases.md` already names a real person, this doc uses that name; otherwise the persona is a composite drawn from the use cases.

---

## 1. The Producer (irregular supply)

- **Who they are:** A maker, grower, fisher, or cook running a one-person operation with non-uniform output. Ferrari (wild-caught fish, irregular catches), the Quarterly Dip Vendor (African-inspired condiments at Sacramento farmers markets, no published schedule), the Food Truck Operator (Sacramento region, no fixed calendar).
- **What they want:** *"Let people find me when I have what I have. I'm not going to maintain a website or update Instagram every Tuesday — but I'd post a one-line update if it took five seconds and reached the people who already chose me."*
- **Where they get stuck today:** Square optimizes for predictable storefronts; Instagram optimizes for the post-after-the-fact; mailing-list tools optimize for newsletters. None of them are shaped for *"salmon today, here, until 4pm."* The text-list-of-customers approach works but doesn't compound — every new customer requires manual onboarding.
- **Use cases they appear in:** [#2 Ferrari Fisheries](use-cases.md#2-ferrari-fisheries), [#3 Quarterly Dip Vendor](use-cases.md#3-the-quarterly-dip-vendor), [#4 Food Truck Without a Calendar](use-cases.md#4-the-food-truck-without-a-calendar).
- **Loops they touch:** 7 (Make and be found), 8 (Follow what you love).

## 2. The Convener (recurring host)

- **Who they are:** Someone who organizes a recurring gathering — usually unofficial, usually word-of-mouth. The Run Club organizer at Drake's, the Barn Movie Night host, the Concerts-in-the-Park series runners, the recurring book-club lead. Brian from F018 is one.
- **What they want:** *"One shareable URL I can text to friends, chalk on a board, or post in any group chat. A page that says when, where, who, and how to show up — without me having to host a Substack."*
- **Where they get stuck today:** The current tools (Facebook Events, Eventbrite, a Squarespace site, a Google Doc) all want them to do marketing they don't want to do. The Run Club has been working for years with no URL; the cost of that to newcomers is silent and large.
- **Use cases they appear in:** [#1 Run Club at Drake's](use-cases.md#1-the-unofficial-run-club-at-drakes), [#5 Barn Movie Night](use-cases.md#5-barn-movie-night-at-drakes), [#12 Concerts in the Park](use-cases.md#12-concerts-in-the-park) (host side).
- **Loops they touch:** 1 (Find your people), 4 (Gather regularly), partially 3 (Land here — the newcomer is the *other* side of this loop, but the convener is the supply).

---

## 3. The Newcomer / Wanderer

- **Who they are:** Someone new to the locality — recently moved, recently returned, recently single, recently retired, recently anything. They have lost their network or never had one here. Adaeze in F025 is the canonical example: returned to her hometown after years away, professionally rooted but personally disconnected.
- **What they want:** *"What is happening near me this week that I could just show up to. I don't want to follow 40 Instagram accounts to find out."*
- **Where they get stuck today:** The discovery cost of recurring real-world life is currently very high. You have to know who organizes what, which Instagram accounts to follow, which Substacks publish a weekend roundup. Most newcomers give up after a few attempts.
- **Use cases they appear in:** [#5 Barn Movie Night](use-cases.md#5-barn-movie-night-at-drakes) (newcomer side), [#12 Concerts in the Park](use-cases.md#12-concerts-in-the-park) (Loop 3 entry), implicit in every gathering example.
- **Loops they touch:** 3 (Land here) primarily, then 1 (Find your people) and 4 (Gather regularly) as they convert.

---

## 4. The Steward / Initiator

- **Who they are:** Someone with the will, the local knowledge, and the social capital to lead a community-scale undertaking — but who lacks the financing, mentorship, or guarantee of community backing on their own. The Cafe Capricho successor candidate is the canonical example. The seed-library founder, the tool-library steward, the repair-café organizer all fit here too.
- **What they want:** *"I'd take this on if I knew the regulars would back me — with patronage, with mentorship, with a few hours of help, eventually with capital. Right now I don't know who they are, and they don't know I exist."*
- **Where they get stuck today:** The aspiring operator and the would-be backers don't find each other. The space gets leased to a chain. The community capacity to act on its own behalf goes uncoordinated.
- **Use cases they appear in:** [#6 Cafe Capricho's Successor](use-cases.md#6-cafe-caprichos-successor); future canonical example #11 (Steward what we built — Loop 12) will fill another shape here.
- **Loops they touch:** 10 (Start something), 11 (Pool resources), 12 (Steward what we built).

---

## 5. The Backer

- **Who they are:** Regulars at a beloved place, neighbors of a beloved space, members of a community who would commit something — money, patronage promises, time, mentorship — if there were a way to do so meaningfully. They are not a customer in the normal sense; they are a stakeholder before the thing they're backing exists.
- **What they want:** *"I'd put down $500 toward someone reopening Capricho. I'd commit to coffee twice a week for six months. I'd put in two Saturdays helping rehab the space. I just need somewhere to say so that means something."*
- **Where they get stuck today:** No primitive exists in the consumer landscape for "pre-commitment to a community-scale undertaking." Kickstarter is the closest analog but it's product-shaped, not place-shaped, and it's national, not local.
- **Use cases they appear in:** [#6 Cafe Capricho's Successor](use-cases.md#6-cafe-caprichos-successor) (the dozens of regulars who would back a successor).
- **Loops they touch:** 11 (Pool resources) primarily; touches 10 (Start something) as the Initiator's counterparty.

---

## 6. The Affinity-Group Seeker

- **Who they are:** Someone who recognizes they share an affinity with others — by interest, life stage, or circumstance — before any specific gathering or shared activity has been declared. The Sacramento women on Bumble BFF looking for friends are the canonical example. The new parents looking for other new parents, the people in recovery looking for sober community, the recent retirees looking for daytime companions — all fit here.
- **What they want:** *"I don't want to host a meetup yet. I just want to declare that this group of us exists, find each other, and let what we do together emerge from there."*
- **Where they get stuck today:** Affinity-recognition tools optimize for one-on-one (dating-app shape) or for closed-group conversation (private Facebook group). Neither gives the affinity a public page anyone could discover by being in the locality and curious.
- **Use cases they appear in:** [#7 Bumble BFF Refugees](use-cases.md#7-the-bumble-bff-refugees). Future canonical examples for Loops 5 + 6 (mutual aid) may surface a variant of this persona organized around offers/asks instead of identity.
- **Loops they touch:** 1 (Find your people) — but from the affinity-first direction, not the gathering-first direction. Once the Group exists, members touch Loops 4, 5, 6 from inside it.

## 7. The Follower (loyalty-shaped)

- **Who they are:** Someone who has chosen a Producer or a Convener and wants to be alerted when they have something to offer. The Ferrari Fisheries customer, the dip-vendor regular, the food-truck stalker, the Run Club Thursday-night returner, the Concerts-in-the-Park enthusiast following a specific park.
- **What they want:** *"When they have something, I want to know. Don't show me every other vendor — show me the ones I've already said yes to."*
- **Where they get stuck today:** Following on Instagram delivers ambient broadcast inside an algorithmic feed designed to capture attention from other accounts. Mailing lists work but require the Producer to maintain them. Text lists work but don't scale.
- **Use cases they appear in:** [#2 Ferrari Fisheries](use-cases.md#2-ferrari-fisheries), [#3 Quarterly Dip Vendor](use-cases.md#3-the-quarterly-dip-vendor), [#4 Food Truck](use-cases.md#4-the-food-truck-without-a-calendar), [#12 Concerts in the Park](use-cases.md#12-concerts-in-the-park) (the multi-Location follow case).
- **Loops they touch:** 8 (Follow what you love); touches 7 (Make and be found) as the Producer's counterparty.

---

## 8. The Everyday Neighbor

- **Who they are:** A Member who lives in the locality, may or may not be actively producing or convening anything, browses the platform occasionally, helps when asked, attends gatherings when they fit, and adds to social capital by showing up. The largest persona by count; the substrate every other persona depends on.
- **What they want:** *"Show me what's going on. Let me say I support someone without writing a review. Let me help if a neighbor asks. Otherwise leave me alone."*
- **Where they get stuck today:** Most platforms force the Everyday Neighbor to actively participate to get any value — post, comment, rate. This Member's job is mostly to *show up*, which the existing tools cannot measure or reward.
- **Use cases they appear in:** Implicit in every use case. Explicit at [#5 Barn Movie Night](use-cases.md#5-barn-movie-night-at-drakes) (the attendees who aren't the host), [#12 Concerts in the Park](use-cases.md#12-concerts-in-the-park) (the audience).
- **Loops they touch:** 3 (Land here), 4 (Gather regularly), 8 (Follow what you love), 1 (Find your people) — almost any loop except the explicitly initiator-shaped ones (10, 11, 12).

## What this draft does not yet do

- **Does not stack-rank the personas.** Different personas matter most at different bundle phases — Producers and Conveners drive b1 supply; Newcomers and Everyday Neighbors drive b1 demand; Initiators and Backers come into focus at b3. PM to decide whether to rank globally or per-bundle.
- **Does not name explicit anti-personas.** The platform deliberately doesn't serve some shapes — the corporate-shell franchise, the rollup-acquirer, the engagement-optimizer. Those refusals live in [`../foundation/principles.md`](../foundation/principles.md) Part 2 (the People-First Principle); whether they need a "who this is *not* for" section here is a PM call.
- **Does not define personas at the Group level.** Groups are sets of People; they don't have their own personas. But the Group surfaces (kind='business' Group steward, kind='interest' Group founder, kind='event_anchored' Group convener) may warrant their own framing if the spec layer goes that direction. **[PM: confirm]** whether Group-role personas belong here or in [`../systems/groups.md`](../systems/groups.md).
