# Product: Business Data

**One-line description:** Storage, registration, and management of business listings with ownership classification

**Hypothesis:** Business owners will self-register if the flow takes under 5 minutes and immediately makes them discoverable to values-aligned customers.

**Bundle Assignment:** b1 (T1), b2 (T2), b3 (T3)

## Tier Summary

### T1 (MVP)
Self-registration form, ownership selector, pin activation, community support + report.
- C1: Business Registration
- C2: Community Signals (Support + Report)

### T2 (Core)
Community-submitted listings, claim flow, categories taxonomy, photos.
- C3: Community Seeding
- C4: Claim Listing

### T3 (Polish)
Flagging/verification, ownership history timeline, PE parent company database.
- C5: Flag Listing
