# Movers, Makers & Shakers — The Constitution

> **Everything serves people.**

That is the whole project.

The platform serves Members. Business serves the people who do the work — not shareholders at their detriment. AI serves the people it acts for — it does not replace them. Data serves the people it describes. Capital serves the people it circulates among.

We measure this as **Member Flourishing**, on two dimensions that must both rise together: *time to live* (discretionary hours, not eaten by work, caregiving, or commute) and *money to live* (disposable income, above the cost of basic needs). Every proposal passes one test: *does this net-move Members up on both?*

The one thing we will not do is support **extraction** — value taken from people without serving them in return. Everything else is open to revision as conditions change.

**People can do more. They don't have to.**
