## Product & platform
- **2026-09-17 — Sample cards carry a mark, and the mark is the last line of defence rather than the first.** Don's ruling, unblocking the example block *(the not-covered-yet state, #149)*. **The reasoning, so it is not relitigated: a stranger scrolling reads shapes, not badges.** Someone skimming registers *"cards of local businesses"* long before reading any label on one, and by then they have already formed a belief about how much is here. **So separation and non-interactivity carry the weight:** examples live in their own bounded block with their own heading, never in the same grid or scroll region as real results, never returned by search and never paginated into; and nothing on an example card behaves like a real Page — no link to a Page URL, no follow or support control, no map pin. The persistent mark and the tinted ground are the third line, not the first. **The caption says it illustrates the idea, not that it samples the stock** — a set of examples spanning categories otherwise reads as a supply claim. **Place names are the real metro being discussed; business names are obviously illustrative rather than plausible local firms**, because a plausible name is indistinguishable from a real listing at a glance and could collide with an actual business. Built in `socialus-web` PR #150.
- **2026-09-17 — Support and get-updates are two different links between the same two things, and the difference is the inbox.** Don: *"Support is a like or a follow. I don't know if there's a difference yet. I just don't want to copy Airbnb exactly which is why the ribbon."* / *"There could be a get updates button. This should be in place of emails from vendors with offers etc."* / *"Where a support is perhaps a softer signal."* **Support (the ribbon)** is one tap, a soft public-ish backing of a Page — *I like that this exists*. **It produces nothing in anyone's inbox: no notification, no alert, no digest, no subscription.** **Get updates** is the actual subscription, and its stated purpose is to **replace promotional email from businesses** — updates arrive in the app, never in a mailbox. Named in words rather than by number because they are two link types, not two buttons: *a Member supporting a Page* and *a Member subscribed to a Page's updates*. Written up in `product/foundation/verbs.md` § Support and get-updates.
- **2026-09-17 — The design consequence that keeps support honest: support generates no notifications, and if it ever does, it has silently become a subscription.** This is what makes the ribbon compatible with the not-addicting constraint rather than a second pull-back loop wearing a softer word. It sits under the standing *no engagement optimization, anywhere* line and under `product/foundation/metrics.md`'s refusal of notification open-rate as a metric. **The test is one sentence: if tapping the ribbon can cause a member's phone to light up later, the ruling has been broken.**
- **2026-09-17 — Get-updates is a privacy posture, not only a feature: a Page owner reaches the people who subscribed without ever holding their contact details.** It answers the escape hatch `product/foundation/messaging-problem.md` names — an organizer publishing a contact address in a public announcement, *"scrapeable, not revocable"* — by giving a route that needs no address at all. **Checked against what is already ruled: it contradicts nothing and reinforces two things** — signup collecting *"nothing more without a stated reason"* (2026-09-14), and the refusal of pull-back notification loops. **One real tension found and NOT resolved here:** the 2026-09-08 line says a business *"may see its own audience"*, while F067's implementation writes followers with `source = 'soft_via_follow'` specifically so the list is unreadable **by anyone, the owner included**. *Reaching people without holding their email* and *reaching people without knowing who they are* are different promises. **Don has to say which one get-updates makes.**
- **2026-09-17 — "Newcomers" on Browse means newly opened Pages, and that lens is load-bearing for supply.** Don's ruling, settling an ambiguity that had three plausible readings: new Pages, new members, or newly listed items. It is **new Pages** — `groups` ordered by `created_at`, scoped to the place, active and listed. **Why it matters beyond being a section:** before anyone follows a brand-new Page, a newcomers lens is the only way that Page is seen at all. It is the structural answer to *"I made a Page and nobody saw it"*, which Don hit himself on 2026-09-16, and it should never be cut as decoration. **The thin-row discipline applies and is not optional here**: a newcomers row on a site with three Pages shows the same three permanently, which stops being news and starts being furniture — so the lens appears only when it has enough genuinely recent Pages to be worth a heading, and withdraws rather than repeating itself. Fourth axis alongside time, product category and cost. Scenario: the newcomer-browses scenario (F059).
- **2026-09-17 — Browse is a showcase, not a catalogue, and it carries a signed-in person's own announcements and upcoming things.** Don: *"The explore page is a showcase and should show the signed in user things like announcements and upcoming things instead of hiding it in /you... explore for people not signed is excludes the personal following things. And search is for looking for something specifically. We should have categories like what's going on today etc. it's a way to change what's discoverable instead of showing the same things to everyone all the time we don't want to be addicting but we also don't want to be boring."* Extended the same day: *"organic local food items or household items or art things etc. or free stuff like dirt or manure"* and *"a horizontal scroll feed where there's additional content off to the right for more choices."* **Four things this settles.** (1) **Curated lenses replace the ban on category controls** — the browse scenario's criterion barring every pill, chip and filter row is reversed. A lens varies what is discoverable; it is not a taxonomy of kinds, and the shipped kind pill row does not survive. Lenses run on **time** (on today), **product category** (local food, household, art) and **cost** (free things) — cost being a distinct axis, and free-to-collect a neighbourhood case the others miss. (2) **Search is a separate job** — finding something specific, not being shown something. (3) **Signed in, Browse carries the person's own announcements and upcoming things; signed out, that half is absent** — and absent **server-side**, never rendered and hidden, because a surface that ships personal content to every reader has already disclosed it. Browse is for discovery; the member's own area is for management. (4) **Not addicting and not boring is a constraint on ranking, not a slogan** — variety comes from rotating lenses, never from optimising for what holds attention. Reinforces the 2026-09-12 foundation wording that ranking may never use what keeps you scrolling. Scenario: the newcomer-browses scenario (F059), amended and approved the same day.
