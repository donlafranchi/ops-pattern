## Product & platform

- **2026-09-13 — A post can be edited after posting. Announcements and events both.** Don: *"they can both be edited or replaced entirely, whatever is easier."* **Reverses "no edit/delete after posting"**, which stood in `nouns.md` (Announcement) and in `verbs.md`'s forbidden list with the reasoning *"a broadcast rewritable after people read it isn't a broadcast."* **That reasoning is set aside, not satisfied** — recorded plainly so nobody re-derives the old rule from it later.
  - **It resolves a contradiction rather than creating one.** `model.md` already said *"editing one occurrence ships… every week differs anyway"*, while `nouns.md` forbade editing any post — and under one-mechanism an occurrence **is** a post. Both were ruled; they could not both hold. This picks editing.
  - **Edit-in-place or replace-the-row is the implementer's call** — *"whatever is easier"*. **With one consequence that is not free, flagged and not decided:** `item_responses` attaches by row id, so **replacing a row discards every response on it.** A member says they are coming, the organizer fixes the start time, and the attendee list empties. **Edit-in-place has no such cost.** If replace is chosen, the responses must move with it, and that is work rather than a shortcut.
  - **Delete is NOT ruled on and stays refused.** Don said edited or replaced; he did not say deleted. For an occurrence it is refused regardless — a deleted occurrence returns on the next nightly top-up, which is why cancellation is a state (F075).
