# DECISIONS.md

One dated line per ruling, newest first. Append only — never edit a past line; a reversal is a new line that says what it replaces. Merges the former `planning/DECISIONS.md` (launch-tier log) and `product/foundation/decisions.md` (foundation set), plus the standing decisions from `playbooks/PLATFORM-PATTERNS.md` and `playbooks/DEVELOPMENT-PATTERNS.md`. Those four files are retired; this is the one home.

## Product & platform

- **2026-09-12 — Agents own whether a change is correct; Don owns whether it is right.** He does not read code and nothing may ask him to. Every PR opens with either "Don doesn't need to look" plus a reason, or a preview link and three plain-language steps written for a phone, labelled `needs-don`. He looks when a change alters what a person sees or does, adds a capability for the first time, touches privacy/money/public visibility, carries a judgment word in its acceptance criteria, or came back with a deviation. He does not look at invisible migrations, tests, refactors, docs, infrastructure or dependency bumps. Nothing blocks a merge — the label stays on afterwards so `is:merged label:needs-don` lists what shipped unseen. Mechanism: `PIPELINE.md` § Who checks what.
- **2026-09-12 — A change that cannot be described in three plain steps needs Don's eyes more, not less.** The PR says so and carries the label anyway. An agent unable to explain a change without jargon has found something worth his attention, not grounds to skip him.
- **2026-09-12 — Responding to an event is a response with a state, one per member per occurrence — not a thumbs up, not a boolean.** The Page owner sees four states: who is coming, who isn't, who has seen it and is undecided, who has declined. *(Whether "isn't" and "has declined" are one state or two was not specified — flagged, not invented.)* Replaces the same-day entry recording it as a thumbs up.
- **2026-09-12 — The response count is public.** Don: people want to know whether an event they are considering will be well attended, and more generally *"let them decide what they like and what's popular for them."* Withholding information from the public by instinct is the thing to resist.
- **2026-09-12 — Correction: showing a count and ranking by it are different things, and only the second is forbidden.** The 2026-09-10 entries treated "never an input to ordering" and "not a social signal" as one commitment. They are not. Displaying a response count on an event is information a person uses to decide, and is fine. **Ordering browse results by it** is what the no-sold-visibility and no-engagement-ranking commitments forbid. The blanket version was an agent's conflation, not a ruling.
- **2026-09-12 — Responses show names and small profile photos**, especially for meetups and group things. Supersedes the 2026-09-10 recommendation of count-only-no-names, and closes the open count-vs-names question from earlier the same day.
- **2026-09-12 — This answers, for responses only, the parked question of whether a Page owner sees who noticed.** Yes for an event response, which is a deliberate declaration a member makes. It does **not** automatically generalise: a page view (F068) is passive and a reaction on an announcement (F066, "a count and no identities, ever") is a lighter act. Both stay as they are until ruled on separately.
- **2026-09-12 — Members need an avatar, and that is new work upstream of responses.** `members.display_name` exists and is always populated. `members.avatar_url` exists as a column and is read by the member page, but **nothing writes it** — there is no upload surface, so every avatar is empty. Names and faces on a response list cannot ship before it does.
- **2026-09-12 — Direct messaging is coming, with safeguards, between creators and followers and within the community.** Don, explicitly. No longer hypothetical and no longer to be written about as merely possible; still unscheduled and undesigned. The safeguards are required, not optional — `product/foundation/messaging-problem.md` is where the reasoning lives.
- **2026-09-12 — A Page is "an organizing entity for something that needs more than one of anything."** Don's words, and now the definition. Long-lived, carrying multiple meetups, announcements, maybe conversations. **Retires "a Page is *who*; an Item is *what*"** — that pairing depended on Items, which no longer exist, and answered the wrong question. Two rules stop needing to be stated separately because they now follow from the test: venues are Pages (long-lived, host many things), and a single occasion is not one (it needs one of everything, so it is a post with a time).
- **2026-09-12 — Venues are Pages, not a separate noun.** Follows from the definition above. Removes `Venue` from the noun set; the "Appearance" noun (a Page at a Venue for a bounded time) is now likely redundant too, since a post carrying its own address does that job — flagged, not ruled.
- **2026-09-12 — A post can carry its own address, separate from its Page's.** How an itinerant Page's event reaches the map at the place it actually happens. Supersedes "a Page with no fixed place is found through the Venues it appears at, never pinned at an address it doesn't have."
- **2026-09-12 — A creator dropping a pin sets its precision; the platform never snaps, rounds or coarsens it.** *(Provisional — read from a transcription artifact, being confirmed.)* The consequence is not provisional: with no guard in the data, the copy is the only guard. A warning at the moment of placement and a confirm-back of the derived place are both **required**, not advisory.
- **2026-09-12 — The retired Item URLs redirect to the originator or creator.** Don's words were "originator/creator", which for a Page-filed thing may mean the creating Member rather than the owning Page — those differ, and the distinction is **not resolved here**. Whichever it is, the old product/service/event URLs resolve rather than 404.
- **2026-09-12 — The browse tickets are closed and reissued, not rewritten in place.** They were written against an Item-indexed read path that no longer exists.
- **2026-09-12 — The photos chain proceeds unchanged.** Page-level work, unaffected by the Item removal.
- **2026-09-12 — Whether the thumbs up shows a count or names is still open.** Don is undecided and wants more to go on. Recorded as open so it is not treated as settled by the surrounding rulings; the deferred "does a Page owner see who noticed" question is the same question in other clothes.
- **2026-09-10 — Browse returns Pages, and posts carrying a date and a place, as first-class results.** Don, confirming: *"You're right to include anything with a date and a location. We use a map to tell someone where to go."* A specific occurrence is its own result at the place it happens — this Saturday's farmers market, not a filter on the market's Page. Settles the one sentence in `model.md` that had been inferred rather than stated.
- **2026-09-10 — There are no Items. What a creator offers is described on their Page and in its posts; it is not a separately listed thing that browse indexes.** Don's ruling, stated directly. Supersedes the Page-is-*who*/Item-is-*what* pairing and the three-noun spine in `product/foundation/nouns.md`, which are now wrong rather than incomplete. Browse finds Pages, and posts that carry a time. Full statement: `product/foundation/model.md`.
- **2026-09-10 — A post with no start and end time is an announcement; the same post with a start and end time is an event. One table (`page_posts`), one composer.** Followers and members receive it either way; the map and date search read the ones with times. That is how an event reaches a map.
- **2026-09-10 — A recurring series generates its occurrences as real rows, on a rolling three-month horizon topped up nightly; cancelling an occurrence is a state on the row, never a deletion.** Rows rather than read-time computation because each occurrence is individually editable — the run club runs a different route every week. The top-up job is idempotent: it inserts only what is missing, so a deleted occurrence would simply reappear the next night. Editing one occurrence ships for launch; editing a series does not.
- **2026-09-10 — The occurrence-generating job is a scheduled function in the app, in TypeScript, under review and covered by tests — not a database job.** The general rule it sets: put logic where it can be tested and reviewed, put constraints where they cannot be bypassed. Generating occurrences is logic; cancellation state is a constraint.
- **2026-09-10 — Responding to an event is back in, as a thumbs up on an occurrence, for a headcount.** Don's ruling, replacing the same-day entry that recorded the cut's reasoning as void with the cut still standing. The thumbs up sits on a post carrying a time — Thursday's run, not the run club, not the Page. It is a count for the organizer: **never an input to ordering anywhere** (no sold visibility, no engagement ranking). Names, messaging attendees, capacity and waitlists are out; an organizer needing more posts an announcement asking people to email a contact.
- **2026-09-10 — Recommended and awaiting Don: the thumbs up shows a count only, with no names, and requires being signed in.** No names keeps the separate, deferred "does a Page owner see who noticed" decision deferred rather than answering it sideways. Signed-in-only is what lets a count be counted once; anonymous browse is unchanged and an anonymous viewer sees the number without being able to add to it.
- **2026-09-10 — Whether venues are Pages too, as places where things happen, is unsettled.** Raised tentatively by Don in the same statement; recorded as an open question rather than as a ruling in either direction.
- **2026-09-10 — Bottom-anchored controls vs. the shipped top-anchored search row: resolved by what shipped.** The top-anchored row (`design-research-thesis.md`, now deleted) stays as a stated, accepted violation for one release (F059); it reverts when the browse-chrome rework lands. Not a standing exception to the bottom-anchor rule.
- **2026-09-10 — Page-view counts to the owner (F068) will eventually show first names/nicknames, not stay counts-only forever.** Ships counts-only at launch; naming is a later increment on the same capture, using the existing free-text `display_name` field — no new column.
- **2026-09-10 — A member outside every seeded metro (F049) picks their metro from a short list of nearby candidates, rather than a coarse rural fallback or a silent null.** E.g. a Truckee resident sees both Reno and Sacramento and picks. Exact selection mechanism (ZIP-distance, state filter, map) is unresolved — decide at ticket time. Rules out: seeding coarser rural metro polygons, and defaulting a rural member to a null or city/county-only vantage point.
- **2026-09-10 — Atomic scenarios replace the long-form scenario/review format; reviews are folded into the scenario's `approved:` line and deleted.** `planning/*.md` is capped at 40 lines, three sections (Story, Acceptance, Not this). Full history of every retired scenario/review lives in git, tag `archive-2026-09` and every commit since.
- **2026-09-08 — A follower of a business Page never sees other followers, and the Page never shows its follower list publicly — to anyone, including anonymous visitors.** A follower list on a business is a customer list, published; the business may see its own audience, nobody else has a reason to. A social group's members list is a different rule for a different noun — members may see each other.
- **2026-09-08 — A follower and a member are the same row (`relationship: 'member' | 'follower'`, defaulted from Page kind), with identical rights today.** The column exists so the two can diverge later without a migration — interest and belonging are different relationships and must not share a row, or the weaker claim inherits the stronger one's access.
- **2026-09-07 — A bulletin is one-way broadcast; a reaction count is shown to the Page owner, never a roster of who reacted.** A count is the audience answering; a roster is surveillance of the audience — same tap, two different products.
- **2026-09-09 — A Page's address is public if given; a neighbourhood is the private-safe alternative.** *(Supersedes the earlier foundation rule "street addresses never appear on public maps" — the newer, PM-instructed ruling wins. The address field must say it's public before anyone types into it.)*
- **2026-09-09 — The message board is `page_posts`, not `bulletins`.** Reply-capable (`parent_post_id`), authored (`author_member_id`), typed (`kind`) from the first migration, so replies and member-authored posts are later increments, not rewrites. Schedule unchanged: audience is members, 2 days, unticketed.
- **2026-09-09 — Browse: the approved Item-based scenario (F059) is rewritten around Pages, not re-numbered.** Its tickets (T127–T131) were built against the superseded Item model; the rewrite is flagged, not yet done.
- **2026-09-07 — Reports write to a table at launch; no SLA, no moderation flow, no operator queue.** Unblocks photo upload. Copy must not imply a response is coming.
- **2026-09-07 — Seed content is synthetic and display-only.** No real-producer recruitment before launch; not reportable as traction.
- **2026-09-07 — The producer values declaration is cut from launch.** The constraint survives: a values statement, whenever built, is self-declared only, never sourced or inferred.
- **2026-09-07 — "Where they'll be next" ships as one free-text line (≤140 chars) on the shop editor, not structured recurring scheduling.** Structure is a priced v2 buy-back; every producer who fills the sentence re-enters it by hand later.
- **2026-09-07 — Exactly three public promises: surplus returns to the community; every decision weighs member benefit against product benefit; not an extractive platform.** Every other prior commitment (never paying for visibility, member ownership, etc.) is withdrawn. Nothing is published until the PM says so.
- **2026-09-07 — A guidelines tier sits below promises.** Two guidelines ratified: visibility isn't sold by default; participation is never gated behind a fee. Two promise candidates (shared ownership, member/community-first responsibility) drafted, not ratified.
- **2026-09-07 — The business claim, not `groups.kind`, is the friction gate.** Anyone may create a Page to host or sell with no ZIP prompt, verification, badge, or lock; those arrive only with a deliberate local-business claim.
- **2026-09-07 — A person holds as many Pages as they have things going on; Page-type conversion is rejected outright.** Different creation flows per type is correct; nothing ever mutates.
- **2026-09-07 — Correction: nothing gates selling.** Any Page may list an Item; a business record is a claim, not a permission. (Corrects a same-day entry that had reinstated the conflation it was meant to remove.)
- **2026-09-07 — Page creation drops the "both sell and host" option; Pages are created sequentially.** A one-line "start another Page" offer at the end of creation is the load-bearing mitigation.
- **2026-09-07 — A Page is who; an Item is what.** One-time events are Items with a date, filed under a Page — no Page is created for a single occasion.
- **2026-09-07 — The map pins Pages, not Items.** Matching Items group by Page per location; one pin per location, popup lists that Page's Items.
- **2026-09-07 — One Page is one place; no map grouping across locations.** A two-location business is two Pages. Introduces "appearances" (a Page appearing at a Venue) as the one new vocabulary word needed.
- **2026-09-07 — The "Active in the community" badge is removed, no replacement.** A badge derived from a held role is the platform ranking people; same shape as the ownership tier already refused.
- **Everything the platform touches serves the people it touches** — a feature whose value to the platform costs a member is out, however well built.
- **Wealth circulates; it is not extracted** — no selling member data, no revenue line that works better when a member is stuck.
- **Paid visibility passes a member-benefit gate; it isn't banned by fiat.** ("It funds the platform" is the product half of the argument and fails alone.)
- **No engagement optimization, anywhere** — no infinite feeds, engagement-ranked sort, streaks, or pull-back notification loops.
- **The platform never rates, ranks, or labels a person** — no star ratings, reviews, trust levels, ownership tiers, or imported reputation.
- **One word for a person — member; every role is derived, never stored.** No account types, no creator/supporter split.
- **A business is the people doing its work — there is no business entity.** No corporate shells, no ownership transfer, no succession.
- **Groups are joined, never assigned** — no geofenced or auto-populated membership.
- **In public, locality is coarse; precision stays private** — no distance-to-you readouts, no location-scoped feeds or messaging. *(See the 2026-09-09 Page-address entry above for the one narrow public-address exception, PM-instructed.)*
- **Sharing is opt-in, granular, visible, revocable** — no pre-checked boxes, no default-on sharing.
- **The platform records facts; it never performs legal acts or speaks legal language.** No entity type, tax status, or legal/tax vocabulary in any user-facing string, ever — the columns may exist as off-platform fact; nothing may ask.
- **No venture capital** — no priced rounds, no plan that only works at venture scale.
- **Measure what happens inside the app; the north star is time and money together.** No claimed economic-impact multipliers.
- **The platform is the technology layer, never the bank.** A chartered partner holds member funds; the platform holds none, stores no card numbers.
- **What an assistant knows belongs to the member; the assistant never holds the keys.** Context is exportable, deletable, never trained on; credentials are minted per turn at the network edge.
- **A point asserts presence; nothing absent may make that assertion.** Position for a Page is resolved at read time from its Items, never stored or cached.
- **Demand is measured before it's built, including our own** — category "Other" text, not-built-yet taps, and idea-floats are all counted, never auto-promoted to a commitment.
- **Member discoverability defaults to private; outputs (Items, Groups) surface on their own settings.** A business Page stays public for commerce; the Member-behind-it stays separately gated.
- **Membership is the only access-granting verb for a business Page.** No ownership transfer, no succession, no role machinery beyond the membership row; owners are co-equal.
- **Business-Page lifecycle has no auto-dormancy or auto-dissolution** — inactivity affects surfacing only; the only end-state is an owner-called dissolve.
- **The platform does not generate QR codes.** Sharing is phone-to-phone. A producer generating their own business QR stays open as an unbuilt, unscoped idea.
- **No legal or tax language reaches a person**, and entity type/state of formation/formation date never surface in any user-facing copy.
- **One route: `_inbox/` → `planning/backlog/` → `next/` → `now/` → `done/`, decisions distill up into this file.** *(Superseded by this revamp — the lane structure is gone; a scenario's frontmatter `status` is now the only state.)*

## Build & stack

- **2026-09-09 — "No registers" is amended: the rule bars hand-maintained indexes people read to find out what is true, not machine-generated data files a script compares.** Don's ruling. What rotted in REGISTRY / MAP / TRACE / STAGE-LEDGER / JOURNAL was being believed while stale; a file nothing reads to be right cannot fail that way, and its drift surfaces as diff noise immediately. The test is who reads it to be correct, not the file format. Replaces the flat prohibition in `CLAUDE.md` § Naming. `accepted-risks/` is in bounds under the amended rule and stops being an exception; `DECISIONS.md` remains the only thing that settles a question.
- **2026-09-09 — The four `member_public_*` SECURITY DEFINER views are accepted risk, not a defect: the advisor ERROR stands and the views stay.** `member_public_group_memberships`, `member_public_has_published`, `member_public_discoverability` and `member_has_standing_presence` run with owner privileges *because that is the mechanism* — each answers one public question over a table whose RLS is owner/co-member only, and the view's WHERE clause is the gate that keeps the rows underneath invisible. Confirmed against the migration comments in `029`/`030`/`038`. Changing any of them breaks `/m/[handle]`. The Supabase advisor cannot see the intent and will report these on every run; that is the advisor being wrong. Evidence and per-view reasoning: `socialus-web` issue #36.
- **2026-09-09 — `public.spatial_ref_sys` having RLS disabled is accepted risk and is not ours to fix.** PostGIS-owned, held by `supabase_admin`, so the `alter table` fails as `postgres`; it holds coordinate-system reference data with no privacy surface. Already allowlisted with that reasoning in `tests/rls-coverage.test.ts`. Don confirmed. Evidence: `socialus-web` issue #36. *(Same call applies to the three `st_estimatedextent` overloads reported at WARN.)*
- **The app ships on Next.js App Router + TypeScript + Tailwind v4 + Supabase (Postgres/Auth/Realtime) + Mapbox GL JS on Vercel, Playwright for evals, Vitest for units.** A stack-row swap needs a new decision; tuning within a row doesn't.
- **Every write flows through a named, validated, transactional action handler that commits the data row and its `*_events` row together**, with `acting_member_id` always set and `via_delegation_id` when an agent acted.
- **`public.members.id = auth.users.id`, and the post-signup trigger is the only path to a Member row.** No admin create-user surface, no seed-script bypass.
- **The data layer was rebuilt clean-slate on Person/Item/Location/Group** — no dual-write, no backfill, no rollback window; justified only because `web/` had no live users or data at the time.
